How the calculation works

Each goal has its own SIP. The calculator first projects the future cost using inflation, then calculates the starting monthly SIP required to reach that goal while increasing the SIP every year. School is modelled differently because money is withdrawn at the beginning of each academic year; graduation, PG and marriage are modelled as a single withdrawal at the goal date.

Illustrative assumptions: figures such as return, inflation and costs are editable and are not guarantees.
